Harvey Pocket on web
Discover a new way to show your social battery, without words

Fig. 01 · Operating it

  1. 01Press the screen to tip it. The far side becomes the ground.
  2. 02Hold and shake. Keep the mouse down and shake it, or hold your phone and shake that.
  3. 03Let go. It fills back in on its own. Nobody needs to reply.

No mouse? Hold the button, or the space bar.

Fig. 02 · Reading the screen

  • Solid. Battery full. Open to whatever the evening brings.
  • Hollowing. Running lower. Company is fine, conversation less so.
  • Outline. Social battery is flat. It refills by itself, given time.

The longer the shake, the thinner the line. The ring around the screen shows how much is left.

Recovery
1 s of shaking : 6 s back (in daily life, up to 18 h)
