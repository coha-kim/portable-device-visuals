Gravity and push (stands in for the motion sensor until the board arrives)

Press on the round display: that point is the origin of the force. Gravity points away from it, so the opposite side becomes the ground; it stays after you let go (stronger the further from the centre you press, up to 0.6 g at the rim). The press also pushes the blobs away: close ones fly off and bounce off the ground, distant ones barely move. Drag to stir. Press near the centre, or use Level, to level the plate.
